# EPIC-11 — Monitor Visual Redesign

## Capability

A complete visual overhaul of the Monitor's five main surfaces. Each screen is redesigned to make status, progress, and focus immediately readable — without relying on documentation or prior knowledge of Meridian.

## Expected outcome

A user opening the Monitor for the first time navigates confidently: the header identifies the project, Setup cards show document health at a glance, Deliverables communicate version and epic progress visually, the Board has designed empty states and clear card hierarchy, and the Story Detail presents structured content instead of raw YAML.

## Out of scope for this epic

- New data, features, or tabs not already in the Monitor
- Tauri/native packaging
- Authoring wizards or export flows
